## The short answer

At Fackt, a product audit is free, fixed-scope projects start from $5,000 and usually take 2 to 6 weeks, and a monthly retainer is $6,900 for a senior designer working through your requests one at a time.

| Option | Price | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free audit | $0 | Finding out what to fix first |
| Project | from $5,000 | A defined piece of work with a clear finish line, like a new dashboard, an onboarding flow or a landing page |
| Retainer | $6,900 / month | Teams shipping every week who need design to keep up |

There are no hidden fees. A retainer can be paused or cancelled any time.

## What changes the price

Three things move the number most: scope, speed and seniority.

**Scope.** The number of screens and flows matters, but so does everything around them. A dashboard is not one screen: it has empty states, loading states, errors, settings and a mobile view. Redesigning a product that already works also takes more care than starting fresh, because real users already rely on it.

**Speed.** A fixed launch date is fine. A very tight one means more work happens at the same time, with less room between rounds of feedback, and that takes more design time each week.

**Seniority.** You can find cheaper design. What a senior designer adds is fewer rounds, better decisions on the hard screens, and files your developers can build without guessing.

## Project or retainer?

**Choose a project** when you know what you need and it has an end: a new feature, a redesign of one flow, a landing page for a launch. You get a clearly defined scope, milestones over 2 to 6 weeks and daily design support while it runs.

**Choose a retainer** when design work never really stops: new features every sprint, small fixes, marketing pages, experiments. You get a senior designer, unlimited requests worked one at a time with a 24h turnaround, weekly check-ins and a dedicated Slack channel. Pause or cancel whenever the roadmap slows down.

Not sure? Start with the [free audit](/audit). Within 2 days you get a video walkthrough and a written audit of what to fix first, and that usually makes the choice obvious.
