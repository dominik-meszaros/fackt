challengeTitle: Every AI voice site looks the same
challenge: |-
  AI voice tools all promise natural, human sounding speech, usually over a purple gradient with a sound wave. Nobody can hear a website, so the claim falls flat.

  The site needed its own character, and it had to work for two buyers at once: creators who want a voice for a video, and developers who want an API for an agent.
approachTitle: Make it feel like a studio
approach: |-
  The whole page borrows from a radio studio. An on air ticker runs along the top with the latency and languages, and the hero is a live waveform with a playhead moving through it, over the headline: the voice layer for every product, script and agent.

  Every section is a numbered take. Take one is the editor: paste a script, pick a voice, press generate. Take two is the voice library, each voice a textured sphere with a play button. Take three is the control room for developers, with 32 languages, 300 ms to first audio and the live counters a contact center cares about.

  The page ends on a big record button: press record. We built it in Framer, then rebuilt it in Webflow as Booth Audio.
