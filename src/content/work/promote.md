challengeTitle: Marketing platforms that feel complex and faceless
challenge: |-
  In a fast-moving digital landscape, many marketing platforms feel overly complex, fragmented and lack a clear personality. That makes it harder for creators and brands to stand out and grow.

  Promote.fun stands for connection, creativity and results. It is not just about reach, it is about building meaningful influence that drives action. The brand needed to simplify how the platform is perceived and reflect its energetic, creator-first ecosystem.
approachTitle: A bold, approachable identity built around a megaphone P
approach: |-
  The goal was a bold and approachable identity that captures growth, creativity and accessibility, and positions Promote as a platform that helps people amplify their reach while staying fun, modern and instantly recognizable.

  The logomark is a stylized "P" that subtly mirrors a megaphone: communication, amplification and reach. Its geometric balance and clean edges express modernity and trust, and the forward-leaning form conveys motion and progress. Space Grotesk carries the type, and a vibrant Promote Green against Modern Charcoal gives the system energy while keeping it performance-driven.

  The identity is grounded in six values: authenticity first, collaboration over competition, creator empowerment, transparent growth, performance-driven impact and innovation in simplicity.
