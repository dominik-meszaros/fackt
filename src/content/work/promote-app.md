challengeTitle: Creators left guessing
challenge: |-
  Promote is a performance-based influencer marketing platform where brands and creators meet. The dashboard is the central hub where creators track their earnings, discover new viral campaigns and follow their growth, bridging high-value brands and creators who want to monetize their social presence.

  Creators struggle with opaque payout structures and uninspiring, manual campaign discovery. Those bottlenecks lead to burnout and no clear financial direction, while brands miss out on consistent, high-quality engagement. Without real-time clarity, creators are left guessing about their performance and earnings.
approachTitle: Content creation as rewarding play
approach: |-
  Promote turns content creation into a "rewarding play" experience through a centralized, real-time financial dashboard. Deep gamification such as XP levels and submission streaks turns routine tasks into a growth journey, and intuitive campaign categorization lets creators spot high-impact opportunities instantly.

  The interface is dark and high-contrast, set in Space Grotesk with Promote Green as the accent, so earnings, streaks and campaign status read at a glance on desktop and mobile.
