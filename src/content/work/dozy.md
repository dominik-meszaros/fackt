challengeTitle: A sleep app you actually want to open
challenge: |-
  Most sleep trackers read like a lab report: dense charts, medical words and a bright screen at 3 am. We wanted the opposite, an app that feels like a calm friend and still tells you something useful every morning.

  One clear answer. A single score from 0 to 100 each morning, with the detail one tap away.

  A character, not a dashboard. A sleepy sloth that mirrors your night and never judges it.

  Private by default. No account and no sign-up, your nights stay on your phone.

  Easy mornings. An alarm that waits for light sleep inside the window you choose.
approachTitle: From a name and a sloth to the App Store
approach: |-
  Name and brand. Dozy, a soft and sleepy name, with a sloth as the hero. The app icon is the sloth hugging its pillow on a night sky. The type pairs Nunito Black for the big friendly lines with Urbanist for the interface, and the palette runs from deep night to a bright purple, with one colour per sleep stage.

  The sloth system. We drew a full set of sloth states, from asleep and groggy to celebrating and waving, and wrote the rules for when each one shows. A priority ladder decides what you see: live tracking first, then one off events, then last night's mood compared with your own average, then the time of day. The sloth is never sad and never blames you.

  Product design. Onboarding with a sky that follows the time of day, the morning score, a minute by minute timeline of every sleep stage, the smart alarm window, short snore clips, insights that find your best bedtime, sleep sounds that fade out and a night card made for sharing.

  Build and launch. We built Dozy as a native SwiftUI app. Sound is analysed on the device, nights can be saved to Apple Health, and every change ships to TestFlight automatically. Then the launch work: App Store screenshots, the listing in English plus nine more languages, and Dozy Pro as a monthly or yearly subscription.
