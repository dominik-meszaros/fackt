challengeTitle: Selling a patient app to the people who never use it
challenge: |-
  A patient app is bought by a health system's executives and IT team, but its value is felt by patients in a waiting room. Most sites in this space show a list of features and a phone mockup, and the buyer has to imagine the rest.

  The page needed to make the buyer feel the patient's side in a few seconds, and still answer the questions IT asks about scale and security.
approachTitle: Tell it as one patient's morning
approach: |-
  The whole page follows Maya through one visit. Forms done in bed at 7:42. Checked in from the car park at 8:51. Called in by name at 9:02. Paid before the parking meter at 9:31. Each moment is a real phone screen, animated as the page scrolls.

  The visual idea is a door: a warm arch frames the hero phone, and the same arch repeats across clinics, the network map and the footer, so "one front door for every clinic" is something you see, not just read.

  After the story, the page turns to the buyer: the same door at every location, 1,400 front doors across the network, and the security that lets IT say yes. We designed it in Figma and built it in Framer.
