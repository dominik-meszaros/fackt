challengeTitle: A trading community that needed a front door
challenge: |-
  ExitLiquidity is a German crypto trading community: 55+ education modules, live signals, smart bots and a private Discord, free to join with a paid upgrade.

  Crypto sites tend to look the same: loud colors, floating coins and promises. The page had to explain a lot of product in one scroll, make the free offer obvious, and still feel like something people want to belong to.
approachTitle: Nine rounds, one Friday at a time
approach: |-
  Every Friday from July to October a new version went out, and colors, layout and voice all moved before the page settled.

  We started wide: three hero concepts (a live price chart, floating coins and a masked face behind circuit lines) and three color worlds (electric indigo, signal red and monochrome). Monochrome won, and the masked face carried into the final page. The feature list became a bento grid with one small illustration per tile.

  Round six changed the voice. "Join the Syndicate. Dominate the crypto grid." Hooded figures, an all seeing eye and Matrix style rain turned a course platform into something people want to join. The pricing card went through eight versions before it became one plan: a 0 euro VIP+ tier with five clear benefits.

  Then the launch: the whole page in German ("Weniger Show. Mehr echtes Trading."), a two step onboarding (Discord login, then linking a BingX ID), an FAQ in the community's own voice, a set of monochrome 3D illustrations, a style system and every section rebuilt for mobile.
