challengeTitle: Confronting the fragmentation crisis in community management
challenge: |-
  Creators were running their business across a fragmented ecosystem: Discord for community, Teachable for courses, and separate tools for billing and analytics. That software patchwork created real friction.

  Cognitive overload. Densely packed interfaces caused feature fatigue, making it hard for creators to focus on essential management tasks and community goals.

  Fragmented insights. Engagement metrics and revenue tracking lived apart, so creators could not see the real-time financial health of their business.
approachTitle: One calm command center for the whole business
approach: |-
  A minimalist command center: a high-contrast, content-first interface that uses purposeful whitespace to remove distractions and streamline creator workflows.

  Unified growth analytics: one hub that merges member activity with profit tracking, with clear data visualisations at a glance.

  The work ran in four stages over seven weeks: discovery and stakeholder audits, information architecture and UX copy, visual exploration with a cohesive design system, then refinement and a pixel-perfect handoff to the development team. The desktop command center was then carried over to a native mobile app, so the community stays active around the clock.
