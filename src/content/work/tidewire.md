challengeTitle: Selling something engineers only notice when it breaks
challenge: |-
  Deploy and observability tools sell something invisible. Nobody wakes up wanting traces, they want to know why checkout got slow at 2 AM and how to fix it before the next call.

  Most devtool sites answer with logos, a dashboard screenshot and "full stack visibility". Engineers skim that in seconds and leave, because it looks like every other tool they already pay for.
approachTitle: Show the moment, then prove it
approach: |-
  We started from the one moment the product exists for: production breaks. The hero is that moment. A p99 latency spike rises out of a landscape of request lines, and a card on top names the deploy behind it, with a rollback button. Before reading a word, an engineer sees what the tool does.

  The rest of the page proves it in the order a skeptical buyer asks. One incident page shows the whole product: the graph, the affected routes, the commit and the rollback. Then pricing in plain terms, a customer story, the changelog and the trust details engineers check for, like OpenTelemetry and SOC 2. Every section has a product moment instead of an abstract illustration.

  We designed it in Figma and built it in Framer, from desktop to phone, and published it as a template, so the same structure can be shipped for a real devtool in days.

  Building a devtool, an API or infrastructure product? Send us your site and we'll show you what your "moment" is, free.
