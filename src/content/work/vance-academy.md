approach: |-
  The look is quiet and expensive: near black, a single gold accent and a serif headline about the 41,000 businesses behind the brand. Two calls to action from the first screen: apply to work together, or watch the free training first.

  A founder file tells the story in four dates, from the first client to the company today. Then four programs, from a beginner course to the inner circle, each with who it is for and what it includes, so buyers pick themselves.
