challengeTitle: A bank that has to feel expensive and safe at once
challenge: |-
  Founders who sell a company or raise a round suddenly have two balance sheets: their own and the company's. Most banks treat them as two strangers, and most fintech sites look like the same purple app with a phone mockup.

  The site had to feel like a private bank, quiet and exclusive, while still showing a modern product: every account in one view, cash that earns on its own and a real person to call.
approachTitle: Precision, not flash
approach: |-
  The page opens on a vault dial that turns as it loads, with a single steel card resting under the headline: wealth, held with precision. No gradients, no confetti, just black, one warm metal tone and a lot of space.

  Three things the bank does well each get a working piece of product: personal and company balances side by side, idle cash sweeping into a 4.1% yield, and the metal card. Then the app itself, one quiet screen for the whole balance sheet, and a real banker with a name, languages and response time.

  Security is shown like a vault, with segregated accounts, hardware keys and quarterly audits. Pricing becomes membership tiers by assets, and the page closes on an application instead of a sign up.

  We designed it in Figma and built it in Framer, from the vault intro to the phone layout.
