challengeTitle: Selling homes that do not exist yet
challenge: |-
  A developer selling off plan asks buyers to commit to a home they cannot walk through. Most developer sites answer with a glossy render, a contact form and "prices on request". Buyers read that as something to hide.

  The site had to make an unfinished tower feel real, and make the decision easy: which floor, which home, what it costs and when the keys arrive.
approachTitle: Show the tower as it really is
approach: |-
  The hero is the building as a site scan: two million points, floor seven highlighted, next to a live sales feed of homes reserved and floors poured. It says the tower is real and moving before anyone reads a word.

  Below it, a floor explorer lets buyers slice the tower, pick a floor and see every home with its rooms, size, balcony and monthly cost. Sizes and prices sit in the open, from the smallest studio to the largest four room home.

  Then the page answers the rest of the buyer's questions in order: a section cut of what the building is made of, the walk to the river, school and station, a monthly progress scan, a record of promised and kept dates, and a calendar to book a walk of the sales suite.

  We designed it in Figma and built it in Framer, from desktop to phone, with the same dark, technical look on every page.
