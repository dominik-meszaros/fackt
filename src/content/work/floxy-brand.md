challengeTitle: Security and performance, without the cliches
challenge: |-
  Floxy is a modern proxy infrastructure platform built for businesses, developers and privacy-conscious users. The goal was a visual identity that communicates speed, trust and digital freedom while staying minimal, timeless and instantly recognizable.

  Most proxy providers lean on generic cybersecurity aesthetics: glowing blue interfaces, shield icons and complex network graphics. The challenge was a distinctive identity that signals security and performance without relying on those visual cliches. The design objective in one line: modern, reliable, fast, technical, minimal, premium and timeless.
approachTitle: Built from pixel-perfect squares
approach: |-
  The primary logo is built with pixel-perfect squares, reflecting precision and the digital nature of the product. The secondary logo is a pixelated "F" with disintegrating squares, a sign of motion and digital transformation that keeps its structure.

  Space Grotesk is the core typeface, paired with a monochrome palette of Shadow Black, Ghost White and subtle grays. Structured layouts, refined typography and minimal design principles carry the identity across web, mobile and print, so every touchpoint feels like the same brand.
