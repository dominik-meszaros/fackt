challengeTitle: Technical, generic and hard to trust
challenge: |-
  Designing for traders meant balancing technical accuracy, credibility and usability. Trading VPS specs are highly technical and hard to digest, the old design lacked a premium, trustworthy feel, the layout did not guide people toward action, and traders are skeptical without guarantees.
approachTitle: Easy to understand, proven to trust
approach: |-
  Every decision in the layout, flow and content followed two priorities. Easy to understand: traders do not have time to dig, so benefits, pricing and process are clear within seconds. Proven to trust: hardware details, performance stats and security features show real reliability.

  We simplified the copy into scannable sections, introduced custom 3D hardware illustrations made in Figma with a dark-tech theme, structured the page as promise, proof, pricing and trust, and added uptime guarantees, DDoS protection, testimonials and FAQs. The process ran from wireframes and three hero variants through section and bento grids, the mobile version and custom Lottie animations for the hero and bento grids, to the final handoff. Geist carries the type with an electric blue accent.
